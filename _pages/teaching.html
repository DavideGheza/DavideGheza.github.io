I like making methods feel approachable. These are the topics I have taught and enjoyed:

  • Analyzing brain signals. Time-frequency analysis of EEG: wavelets, spectral power, and how to go from raw data to something you can use to answer questions.
  • Computational models of cognitive processes. Reinforcement learning, evidence accumulation and neural network models: how to build a model of behavior and test whether the brain works the same way.
  • Emotion, motivation and the body. Affective neuroscience and psychophysiology: how reward, effort and mood show up in brain and bodily signals.
